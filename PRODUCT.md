# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon builders and demonstrators who need to show convincingly human typing in another macOS application. The primary workflow is composing or pasting source text, choosing or learning a typing profile, arming the app, switching to a target field, and letting the text play back.

## Product Purpose

Typer learns the cadence, corrections, hesitation, and recurring slips in a person's typing samples, then uses that profile to produce a natural-looking keyboard performance. Success means the output finishes accurately while the path to it contains believable pauses, bursts, mistakes, backspaces, and occasional delayed repairs.

## Brand Personality

Focused, clever, kinetic. The product should feel like a serious instrument with a mischievous edge, not a novelty prank or a generic AI dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized SaaS cards, purple gradients, fake analytics dashboards, hacker-movie neon overload, skeuomorphic keyboards, and settings screens that expose every parameter before the user understands the primary workflow.

## Design Principles

- Make the arm-switch-type sequence unmistakable.
- Show the behavior, not just settings: preview the generated keystroke timeline before launch.
- Personalization should feel earned through recorded samples and transparent measurements.
- Keep advanced realism available without burdening the default workflow.
- Always preserve a fast, obvious emergency stop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Respect reduced-motion preferences, never rely on color alone for state, and keep labels visible for every control. Training text remains local to the device.
