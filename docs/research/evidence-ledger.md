## Search record and reconciliation

- Discovery lanes: Aalto rollover/movement papers, distribution-shape paper, POHMM smoothing and TypeNet; CMU definitions/splits, correction paper, SciPy descriptive metrics, synthetic benchmark preprocessing/licenses.
- Coordinator: Apple keyboard/Unicode APIs; independently read CMU, SciPy, Mendeley, shape full text and author-hosted motor/POHMM papers.
- Follow-up resolved “flight” ambiguity, correction publication year (2022), study-specific error priors, dataset-vs-article licenses, exact session split, missing-value treatment and context caveats.
- Remaining empirical gap: no new human capture sessions in this task; no realized cross-app timing receiver run. No scientific improvement claim is made.
- Stop rationale: all consequential implementation choices have primary support or explicit engineering/empirical limitations; more broad searches would not resolve the need for new human traces and receiver measurements.

## Engineering choices requiring calibration

Pseudocounts 120/48/24; per-session pool cap 128; per-profile pooled reservoir 512; at most 256 exact pairs; generator hold 20–250ms; extractor hold 10–500ms, DD 15–2500ms; pause threshold 1000ms and cap 60000ms; burst threshold 310ms; four-session minimum and two held-out sessions; log-normal scale parameters; conservative modifier/Unicode/edit barriers. None is presented as a published universal optimum.
